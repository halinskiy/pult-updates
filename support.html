Pult Support

Pult turns your iPhone and Apple Watch into a remote for your Mac: a touchpad, a keyboard, media controls, a view of the Mac's screen, and a wrist pointer with pinch to click.

Getting started

  1. Install Pult on the Mac you want to control: download the latest version. It lives in the menu bar.
  2. Allow Pult in System Settings, Privacy and Security, Accessibility (to move the cursor). Screen Recording is only needed to see the Mac's screen.
  3. Open Pult on your iPhone on the same Wi-Fi network, allow Local Network, pick your Mac and enter the code it shows.
  4. Apple Watch: open Pult on the watch while the iPhone is connected. In Pointer, set up the pinch once.

Common questions

The Mac does not appear. Both devices must be on the same network, and Local Network must be allowed for Pult in the iPhone's Settings, Privacy and Security, Local Network.

The cursor does not move. Allow Pult in Accessibility on the Mac, then quit and reopen Pult.

Pinches are missed or click by themselves. Run "Set Up Pinch" again on the watch, and try the sensitivity options.

Contact

Use "Report a Problem" in the app settings, or open an issue at github.com/halinskiy/pult-updates/issues.

Поддержка Pult

Установите Pult на Mac (последняя версия), разрешите ему Универсальный доступ. Откройте Pult на iPhone в той же сети Wi-Fi, разрешите доступ к локальной сети, выберите Mac и введите код. На часах один раз настройте щипок в Указке.

Вопросы и ошибки: «Сообщить о проблеме» в настройках приложения или github.com/halinskiy/pult-updates/issues.
